Privacy Policy – Oceans Across

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Oceans Across respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you communicate with us through our website, WhatsApp, Meta platforms, advertisements, enquiry forms, or other communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, phone number, email address, city, educational background, qualification, preferred study destination, visa-related enquiry details, and other information you choose to provide during an enquiry or consultation.

When you contact us through WhatsApp, we may receive your WhatsApp phone number, profile information made available through WhatsApp, message content, and information necessary to respond to and manage your enquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, provide education and visa-related guidance, manage customer communication, assign enquiries to authorised team members, arrange follow-ups, improve our services, and maintain records related to customer enquiries.

3. WhatsApp and Meta Integration

Oceans Across may use Meta and WhatsApp Business Platform services to receive and manage customer enquiries. Information received through these services may be processed by our authorised systems and team members for customer support, enquiry management, and follow-up purposes.

Our use of information received through Meta technologies is subject to applicable Meta platform requirements as well as this Privacy Policy.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with authorised Oceans Across personnel, service providers necessary to operate our communication systems, or other parties when required by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. Access to customer information is limited to authorised personnel where reasonably necessary.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for enquiry management, customer support, business record keeping, legal requirements, and the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact Oceans Across to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by us, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements. You may also ask us to stop non-essential communications.

8. Data Deletion Requests

If you would like Oceans Across to delete personal information associated with your enquiry or communication, please contact us using the contact details below and state that you are requesting deletion of your data. We will review and process valid requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

Our services may use third-party technology providers, including Meta and WhatsApp. These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
